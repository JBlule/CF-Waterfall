Assumptions

What this model deliberately leaves out

This is a teaching tool, not a transaction model. To keep the cascade clear and its results easy to check against a reference spreadsheet, several real-world features are simplified away on purpose. They are listed here so nothing is hidden.

Purpose

The goal is a visual aid for understanding project-finance mechanisms — built around an illustrative motorway-project cash flow. It is not meant to answer a tender or to run an actual project company. Correctness of the modelled mechanics comes first.

Assumed simplifications
  • No circularity Interest, taxes and reserves are computed in a single top-to-bottom pass. There is no iterative loop where a figure depends on a result that depends back on itself.
  • Simplified corporate tax Tax is approximated with a straightforward calculation rather than a full fiscal model (no detailed timing, carry-forwards or deferred tax).
  • No VAT Value-added tax is ignored entirely; all figures are treated as VAT-free.
  • No legal reserves for dividends Distributions are not constrained by statutory or legal reserve requirements — only by the waterfall's own reserve accounts and the lock-up test.
Scope of the current version

Version 1 covers the core mechanics: priority of payments, the MRA and DSRA reserve accounts, the distribution lock-up test and debt sculpting to a target DSCR — run across multiple periods.

Features such as variable-rate debt with an interest-rate swap, sensitivity analysis (tornado charts) and a treasury-management game layer are out of scope until the core is complete and verified against the reference spreadsheet.

Everything runs locally and deterministically: no AI at runtime, no network calls, no external dependencies. The same assumptions always produce the same cascade.
