Definitions

The concepts this tool makes visible

A short glossary of the project-finance mechanisms the cascade is built to explain. Everything below maps directly to a compartment or a rule you can watch on the simulation page.

Cash-flow waterfallpriority of payments

The fixed order in which a project's cash is allocated each period. Cash enters at the top and can only reach a lower compartment once every compartment above it has been served in full. This ordering is the heart of the model.

CFADScash flow available for debt service

Operating revenue minus operating costs (and any prior claims). It is the cash the project actually has available to pay lenders, and the base on which debt service is sculpted.

Debt sculptingDSCR-driven

Rather than a flat repayment profile, scheduled debt service is shaped period by period so that the debt service cover ratio (CFADS ÷ debt service) tracks a target. Repayments are larger when cash is abundant and smaller when it is scarce.

Debt service reserve accountDSRA

A reserve funded up to a target — typically a number of months of upcoming debt service. It is filled before cash is allowed to flow downstream, so lenders keep a buffer even if a period underperforms.

Maintenance reserve accountMRA

A reserve set aside for future major maintenance. Like the DSRA, it must be funded to its target before distributions can be made.

Distribution lock-up testcovenant

A check performed before paying shareholders. If a ratio — for example the DSCR — falls below its threshold, the test fails: distributions are locked up and the cash is trapped in the structure (held in reserves or used to pay down debt) instead of leaving it.

Distributionsresidual

The cash that reaches shareholders, but only after operating costs, debt service, taxes and reserve funding have all been satisfied and the lock-up test has passed.

Cash brought / carried forwardperiod link

Whatever cash remains at the end of a period is carried forward and becomes the brought-forward balance at the start of the next one — the way we move from one column of the model to the next.
