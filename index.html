Cash-flow waterfall

cash is water — watch it fall through the structure, one period at a time
Glossary

What this is. A simulation of how cash circulates through a motorway project company — a special-purpose vehicle that borrows to build a road, then collects tolls and pays everyone back in a fixed order of priority. Cash is water: it is poured in at the top of the cascade and every claim on it is met, or not, as it falls. Whatever reaches the bottom belongs to the shareholders.

The plumbing is drawn in the manner of the MONIAC — the Phillips Machine, a hydraulic computer built in 1949 that modelled a national economy as water flowing between perspex tanks. Press Run the cascade to watch the years go by, or Step to walk through a single year one stage at a time. Click any tank or any pipe to find out what it is.
