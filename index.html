The Cash Flow Cascade

A simplified cash-flow cascade you can watch run — cash is poured in at the top and falls, compartment by compartment, through a strict order of priority.

Inspired by the MONIAC hydraulic economics machine, this simulator turns an abstract cash-flow waterfall into something you can watch flow. Cash enters at the top and cascades through operating costs, debt service, reserve accounts and distributions — each compartment filling only when the ones above it have been served.

This is a teaching tool, built purely for educational purposes. Its aim is to make financial mechanisms such as covenants concrete and easy to see: when a coverage ratio falls below its threshold, cash is trapped and redirected instead of distributed, and you can watch that rule take effect step by step.

Everything is deterministic: the same inputs always produce the same cascade.
